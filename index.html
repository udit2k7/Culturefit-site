CULTUREFIT is an internal analytics and optimization application operated by Madhurm Creations for its own Amazon India selling business. It is being developed to improve visibility across advertising, listings, inventory, finance and marketplace performance.

Private internal application • India

About CULTUREFIT

CULTUREFIT is being developed as a private marketplace-management intelligence layer for Madhurm Creations. Its purpose is to combine authorized Amazon business data with internal business inputs so marketplace decisions can be made with better context and stronger controls.

The current implementation is intended only for Madhurm Creations' own authorized Amazon accounts. It is not presented as an Amazon-owned product, an Amazon endorsement, or a public Amazon integration for third-party sellers.

What we are building

Advertising intelligence

Campaign reporting, keyword and search-term analysis, budget and bid insights, placement analysis and controlled optimization.

Listing intelligence

Listing health, catalog signals, content quality, keyword coverage and controlled optimization recommendations.

Inventory & FBA

Inventory visibility, stock-risk analysis and fulfillment-aware marketplace decision support.

Finance & profitability

Settlement, fee, refund and reimbursement analysis with SKU-level contribution and profitability logic.

Marketplace performance

Sales, pricing, offer and account-performance analysis presented through an action-oriented dashboard.

Controlled automation

Recommendations begin in read-only/shadow mode. Production actions are enabled only after validation and explicit authorization.

Security and data use

Amazon information is intended to be accessed only through authorized Amazon interfaces or reports supplied by an authorized Madhurm Creations user. CULTUREFIT is designed around least-privilege access, secure credential handling, auditability and data minimization.

Buyer personally identifiable information is not required for the application's intended analytics and optimization functions. Access to production write operations is controlled separately from analytical access.

CULTUREFIT is an independent application operated by Madhurm Creations and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Amazon.
